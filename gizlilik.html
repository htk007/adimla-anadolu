Kısaca

Adımla Anadolu kişisel verilerinizi hiçbir sunucuya göndermez; hiçbir veri bize ulaşmaz. Sunucumuz, kullanıcı hesabı, reklam, analitik ya da üçüncü taraf hizmeti yoktur. Uygulamanın kullandığı her bilgi yalnız sizin cihazınızda (iPhone ve eşleşmiş Apple Watch'unuzda) işlenir.

Privacy Policy — Adımla Anadolu

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short

Adımla Anadolu doesn't send your personal data to any server; no data reaches us. There is no server, no user account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party service. Everything the app uses is processed only on your device (your iPhone and paired Apple Watch).

Data controller

Hekantic (Hasan Kaya) — contact: hasankaya@hekantic.com

What is processed on your device

Data Why How
Walking distance and step count (Apple Health) To show your progress along the virtual route If you allow it, it is read from Health and shown on screen; the app doesn't store it. The app never writes to Health.
Location To give you a passport stamp when you tap "I'm here" at a stop Read once, only when you tap; not stored.
Location (optional) If you turn on "notify me when I arrive" in Settings, to know you've reached a stop Used only on your device through iOS region monitoring; not stored or shared. Stops when you turn it off.
Journey dates, unlocked cards, badges, stamp dates, daily streak To show your journey and collection Stored only on this device; deleted when you delete the app.

Apple Watch: the iPhone app sends a summary of your active route (route name, distance walked, next stop) directly to your watch over Apple's iPhone–Watch connection. It never passes through us. The watch app also reads walking distance from Health, only to display it.

Photos

If you tap "Save Image", the share card is added to Photos. The app only asks for "add" permission and can't see your photos.

Notifications

Reminders and "you've arrived" notifications are local notifications created on your device; nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Purchases and restores are handled by Apple (App Store). Your payment details and Apple ID never reach us; the app only learns from Apple which content is unlocked.

Third parties

There are no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party services. Your data is not shared with anyone and not transferred abroad.

Your rights under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698, Article 11)

You may contact the data controller to: learn whether your personal data is processed; request information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties it is transferred to in Turkey or abroad; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request erasure or destruction; request that third parties be notified of corrections or erasure; object to an outcome against you arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to apply: email hasankaya@hekantic.com. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. Because none of your data reaches us, we hold no records about you; you can erase everything on your device by deleting the app, and withdraw Health and location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from children; it doesn't collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.
